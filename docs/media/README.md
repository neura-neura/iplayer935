# Promotional images

These two compositions were made from six user-provided screenshots with the built-in image generation/editing tool. They are promotional composites, not unmodified screenshots. Private profile/folder names and the real server address, share and username are blurred. The public fictional example remains visible. Unredacted originals are not distributed.

Prompt brief: compose three complete app screenshots per landscape banner on an off-white background with coral accents and restrained shadows; preserve the app interface and blur all private identifiers to unreadable smears. First banner: Now Playing, Your Library, Crossfade, with “Your music. Your server. Your iPod.” Second banner: Playlists, Offline Listening, SMB Servers, with “Organize. Download. Take it with you.” Both are titled “iPlayer935”.

Song titles and album artwork belong to their respective owners and are shown as part of the app interface.
